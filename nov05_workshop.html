Opening

Three Principles to Work With

5 min

On Tuesday we sorted retrofits from designs. Today you all redesign. The CAST UDL Guidelines give us three principles to name each change by. Oswal's chapter, in Hewett & DePew's Foundational Practices of OWI, brings them into online writing courses.

Principle 1
Multiple Means of Engagement
The why of learning. Example: students choose a topic tied to their own communities.
Principle 2
Multiple Means of Representation
The what. Example: a reading offered as text, audio, and a one-page outline.
Principle 3
Multiple Means of Action & Expression
The how. Example: a reflection submitted as writing, a voice memo, or an annotated image.

Accessibility Audit

28 min
01
Which elements assume one kind of learner?

Audit your own syllabus or assignment, or use the sample below. Analog Mark up a printed copy and copy the grid onto paper. Online Add the grid as a table at the end of your doc.

Sample syllabus ENG 101 · Course Policies

Attendance. More than three absences lowers your final grade by one full letter. Late arrivals count as half an absence.

Participation. Participation means speaking in every class discussion.

Late work. Late work is not accepted without documentation.

Format. Submit all papers as printed copies at the start of class, typed in Times New Roman 12 pt, double-spaced.

Accommodations. Students with disabilities should contact the Disability Resource Office.

ElementAssumption about the learnerUDL principleRedesign
Worked example: "Participation means speaking in every class discussion." Students show engagement by talking aloud, in real time, in a group. Action & Expression "Participate by speaking in class, posting to the discussion thread before or after class, or adding to our shared notes doc. Each counts the same."
  • 1
    Solo (14 min): Find three elements and fill one row for each. Write the redesign in the words a student would read.
  • 2
    Pairs (14 min): Trade grids. For each redesign, your partner asks: Does this remove the barrier for everyone, or move it somewhere else? Revise one row. Star your strongest revision for the gallery.

OWI and Neurodiversity: A Scenario

22 min
02
What does this online module make easier, and what does it make harder?
Scenario ENG 101 Online · Moodle · Module 4

Module 4 opens Monday. It holds a 40-minute recorded lecture with auto-generated captions, and a single list of 12 linked readings and web pages. A discussion post is due Wednesday at 11:59 p.m., with two replies due Friday. Peer review happens Thursday in a live Zoom session, cameras on, in timed breakout rooms the instructor assigns. Changes to the schedule go out as Moodle announcements.

  • 1
    Pairs (10 min): List affordances and barriers. Read the module from the position of a range of students, for example: someone who processes spoken information slowly, someone who uses a screen reader, someone managing several overlapping deadlines, someone for whom live conversation with strangers costs a lot of energy. Note where one student's affordance is another's barrier.
  • 2
    Pairs with Oswal (4 min): Find one passage in Oswal's chapter naming a barrier you see in this module. Quote it.
  • 3
    Full class (8 min): What does online instruction offer neurodivergent and disabled writers here? Recorded lectures let students pause and replay, for one example. What did the design take away? Name one change, and the UDL principle behind it.

One revision, three lines

Analog Post your starred revision from Activity 01 on chart paper or the whiteboard, in three lines.

Example post Original assumption: engagement means talking aloud in real time.
Principle: Multiple Means of Action & Expression.
Revision: three equal ways to participate, spoken, posted, or added to shared notes.
  • 1
    Post (3 min).
  • 2
    Walk and read (6 min): Leave a sticky note on two posts: "I'd borrow this for…" plus the course where you would use it.
  • 3
    Connect (3 min): Back at your seat, write one sentence linking your revision to your teaching philosophy. It belongs in your final Digital Portfolio.
