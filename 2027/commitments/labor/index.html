Class size is a pedagogical and labor condition

What it means for teaching

CCCC recommends 15 or fewer students per writing class, a maximum of 20, and no more than 60 writing students per instructor per term. Enrollment, assignment quantity and genre, feedback expectations, modality, staffing, accessibility obligations, and compensation must be treated together.

Its limit

These figures are norms and policy recommendations grounded in workload analysis and professional standards. They do not claim learning becomes impossible at student 21.

Register and kind of evidence

Register. Method: how writing produces knowledge.

Kind of evidence. Professional norm, plus one experiment in online courses finding little average effect.
