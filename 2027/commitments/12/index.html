Commitment 12 of 12 · Activity and pedagogy registers

Writing and communication are multimodal and technologically mediated

What it means for teaching

Writing takes print, digital, visual, audio, spatial, data-rich, and networked forms. Instruction addresses how modes, platforms, tools, accessibility, circulation, and audiences reshape rhetorical decisions.

Its limit

The commitment should not be technologically deterministic, and multimodal work is not inherently engaging or equitable. Students need access, accessibility support, rhetorical purpose, and critical attention to the political economy and data practices of platforms.
