Commitment 2 of 12 · Activity register

Writing is a social practice, not merely an individual cognitive skill

What it means for teaching

Writing is learned through participation in communities: feedback, reading, collaboration, uptake, and institutional practice. Instruction attends to discourse communities, genres, activity systems, and relations of power.

Its limit

“Discourse community” is easily pictured as more coherent, more stable, and more equally accessible than any community is.
