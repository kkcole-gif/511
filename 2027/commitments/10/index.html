Commitment 10 of 12 · Activity and method registers

Reading, research, writing, and citation are intertwined literacy practices

What it means for teaching

Academic and professional writing entails evaluating sources, tracing conversations, representing the work of others, making evidence-based claims, and signaling relationships among texts. Citation is an epistemic and ethical practice as well as a format.

Its limit

Citation regimes also enact exclusion, Eurocentrism, gatekeeping, and uneven access to scholarly publication.

Register and kind of evidence

Register. Activity and method: what writers do and how writing produces knowledge.

Kind of evidence. Normative and historical for citation as a practice. Causal for the link between reading and writing, tested with school-age writers. Observational for unequal credit in citation.
