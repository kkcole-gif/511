Condition 1 of 3 · Pedagogy and method registers

Assessment is local, multiple, and purpose-driven

What it means for teaching

The field rejects establishing writing quality through a single timed sample, a universal rubric, a standardized test, or an error count. The alternative is contextual, transparent, multiple, and equitable assessment, designed locally for stated learning goals.

Its limit

The alternative is not the absence of standards. Rubrics clarify values, and they falsely imply writing decomposes into context-free traits.

Register and kind of evidence

Register. Pedagogy and method: how writing is taught and how writing produces knowledge.
