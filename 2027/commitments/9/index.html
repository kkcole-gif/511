Commitment 9 of 12 · Method register

Transfer is possible but not automatic

What it means for teaching

Students carry forward genre awareness, rhetorical concepts, strategies, habits of reflection, and prior writing knowledge, and they must recognize when and how these apply in a new situation. Reflection, comparison, metacognition, and explicit attention to differences across contexts support transfer.

Its limit

Transfer should not be framed as a student deficit. A new discipline, genre, modality, language practice, or institutional setting often demands new learning.
