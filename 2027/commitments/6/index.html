Commitment 6 of 12 · Method register

Writing develops through use in consequential contexts

What it means for teaching

Students learn genres by using them in meaningful disciplinary, civic, professional, and public situations, not by memorizing templates. Disciplinary faculty hold essential expertise in how their fields make and communicate knowledge.

Its limit

Authenticity is not automatic, and simulated genres retain value. Workplace genres should not be imported uncritically, especially where they reproduce exploitative labor norms.
