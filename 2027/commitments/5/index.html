Commitment 5 of 12 · Activity register

Revision is re-seeing, not merely proofreading

What it means for teaching

Revision includes reconsidering a writer's questions, purposes, organization, evidence, warrants, audiences, genre choices, and stakes. Editing matters, and it is one dimension of revision.

Its limit

Not every assignment merits multiple drafts. Mandatory revision without usable feedback, time, choice, or changed evaluative conditions becomes compliance work.
