Commitment 8 of 12 · Method register

Writing development is lifelong and context-dependent

What it means for teaching

No single course teaches students to write once and for all. Programs distribute meaningful writing experiences across curricular pathways, with increasing complexity and opportunities for transfer.

Its limit

The corrective to the one-course solution should not become an unfunded mandate for all faculty. Institutions must provide curricular time, faculty development, writing support, equitable workload, and sustained infrastructure.
