Commitment 4 of 12 · Activity register

Writing is recursive, developmental, and processual

What it means for teaching

Planning, drafting, researching, reading, composing, revising, editing, and reflecting are interdependent, not linear stages. Courses create time and structures for substantive revision.

Its limit

“Process” does not reduce to a uniform sequence. Process pedagogy has been criticized for treating writing as individual and interior and for neglecting genre, history, ideology, institutional constraint, and unequal access to time and feedback.
