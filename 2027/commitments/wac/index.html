Writing belongs throughout an education: WAC, WID, and CAC

What it means for teaching

Writing is integral to learning throughout an undergraduate education and is not confined to required writing courses. First-year composition, WAC and WID, CAC, libraries, writing centers, and disciplinary programs are connected without being collapsed into one another.

Its limit

Faculty development does not resolve structural problems. Curricular change depends on chairs, deans, scheduling, enrollment policy, promotion and tenure, staffing, and budget.
