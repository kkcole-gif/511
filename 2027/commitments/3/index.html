Writing is a mode of learning and inquiry

What it means for teaching

Low-stakes and high-stakes writing help students formulate questions, represent and connect ideas, test claims, reflect, synthesize evidence, and revise their understanding. Writing belongs across the curriculum, in every discipline.

Its limit

Writing does not automatically produce learning. Its epistemic value depends on assignment design, scaffolding, response, revision, and material conditions. The 48-study meta-analysis by Bangert-Drowns, Hurley, and Wilkinson (2004) found a small positive average effect, not a universal one.
