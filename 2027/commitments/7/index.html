Commitment 7 of 12 · Method register

Disciplines are ways of knowing, doing, and writing

What it means for teaching

Writing is constitutive of disciplinary activity: a lab report, a design brief, a case analysis, a proof, a policy memo, and an ethnography do different intellectual work. Carter (2007) calls writing a “metadoing,” a practice giving form to disciplinary ways of doing and knowing.

Its limit

The claim should not reify disciplines, which contain competing methods, genres, ideologies, and publics and which change over time. Instruction in disciplinary writing should teach students to analyze and negotiate disciplinary norms, not only to assimilate.
