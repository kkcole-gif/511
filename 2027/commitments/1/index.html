Commitment 1 of 12 · Pedagogy register

Writing is rhetorical and situated

What it means for teaching

Writers make choices in relation to purpose, audience, genre, medium, evidence, occasion, and institutional consequence. Teaching makes those conditions visible and does not treat “good writing” as universal correctness.

Its limit

Situating writing should not mean teaching accommodation to dominant institutional norms alone. Critical, translingual, antiracist, feminist, disability, and decolonial scholarship asks whose rhetorical norms are treated as normal.
