Commitment 11 of 12 · Pedagogy register

Language difference is a resource, not proof of deficiency

What it means for teaching

Students bring linguistic, rhetorical, cultural, technological, and literacy resources to college. Inclusive writing instruction treats multilingualism and language variation as central to learning while making institutional expectations explicit.

Its limit

The commitment requires more than welcoming language. It entails revising the assessment, feedback, placement, curriculum, and professional-language standards which penalize racialized and multilingual writers. The CCCC Principles reject instruction which standardizes and excludes.
