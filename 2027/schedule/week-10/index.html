The commitment this week tests

Commitment 10 · Activity and method registers

Reading, research, writing, and citation are intertwined literacy practices

What it means for teaching. Academic and professional writing entails evaluating sources, tracing conversations, representing the work of others, making evidence-based claims, and signaling relationships among texts. Citation is an epistemic and ethical practice, not a formatting exercise.

Its limit. Citation regimes also enact exclusion, Eurocentrism, gatekeeping, and uneven access to scholarly publication.

Kind of evidence. Normative and historical for citation as a practice. Causal for the link between reading and writing, tested with school-age writers. Observational for unequal credit in citation.

Reflection Prompt

Citation is an epistemic and ethical practice, not a formatting exercise. Logan shows whose rhetorical education entered the record. Royster asks who speaks with authority about a community's knowledge. Whose work do your own reading lists credit, and what kind of evidence would show a citation practice excludes?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
