In class · Thursday, September 2 · Week 2

Seminar discussion

No separate activity this Thursday. The room works the Reflection Prompt against Berlin (1988) and Pratt (1991).

Matched to Commitment 1: Writing is rhetorical and situated.

Bring

  • Your Perusall annotations for Thursday's readings.
  • One passage you want the room to read closely.
  • One question you were unable to answer alone.

How the discussion runs

Three moves, in order. No clock.

  1. 1

    Move 1. Start from the prompt

    Write for three minutes in answer to the Reflection Prompt. Then say your answer to the person beside you.

  2. 2

    Move 2. Place each reading

    Fulkerson and Berlin sort the field's teaching by what it values. Name the register each reading works in and the kind of evidence it offers. Point to the passage where you see it.

  3. 3

    Move 3. Take the limit seriously

    Read the limit of Commitment 1 aloud. When a classroom names a rhetorical situation, whose norms does it treat as the default? Find the place in Pratt where the question applies.

Reflection Prompt

Fulkerson and Berlin each sort the field's teaching by what it values. Which register are they working in, and what kind of evidence do they offer? Then take the limit seriously: when a classroom names a rhetorical situation, whose norms does it treat as the default?

You leave with one sentence: the claim, its register, and the kind of evidence behind it.
