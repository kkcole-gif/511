The commitment this week tests

Commitment 1 · Pedagogy register

Writing is rhetorical and situated

What it means for teaching. Writers make choices in relation to purpose, audience, genre, medium, evidence, occasion, and institutional consequence. Teaching makes those conditions visible and does not treat “good writing” as universal correctness.

Its limit. Situating writing should not mean teaching accommodation to dominant institutional norms alone.

Kind of evidence. Normative and theoretical.

Open the Commitment 1 page

Reflection Prompt

Fulkerson and Berlin each sort the field's teaching by what it values. Which register are they working in, and what kind of evidence do they offer? Then take the limit seriously: when a classroom names a rhetorical situation, whose norms does it treat as the default?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
