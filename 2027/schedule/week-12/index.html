Condition · Pedagogy register, at the scale of a program

Writing belongs throughout an education: WAC, WID, and CAC

What it means for teaching. Writing is integral to learning throughout an undergraduate education and is not confined to required writing courses. First-year composition, WAC and WID, CAC, libraries, writing centers, and disciplinary programs are connected without being collapsed into one another.

Its limit. Faculty development does not resolve structural problems.

Kind of evidence. Program evaluation and self-report.

Open the page for the WAC, WID, and CAC condition

Reflection Prompt

Faculty-development research rests heavily on self-report and voluntary participation. North argues from theory. Salazar, in the supplements, reviews 82 quantitative studies of writing-center visits. What kind of evidence would persuade a dean, and what kind is honest to offer?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
