In class · Thursday, November 11 · Week 12

Design charrette

You design one faculty-development session or one writing-center training module for a named department, in one sitting, and the room responds.

Matched to the WAC, WID, and CAC condition: Writing belongs throughout an education: WAC, WID, and CAC.

Suggested form. This week's Workshop Team designs and leads the session, and the team is free to adapt every step.

Bring

  • A pen or markers.
  • The team supplies poster paper and names the department.

The session, step by step

Six steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

123456
  1. 1

    Step 1. Hear the brief

    5 min11:45 to 11:50

    The team names the department and the problem its faculty report.

  2. 2

    Step 2. Draft the design

    25 min11:50 to 12:15

    In groups of three or four, design a 60-minute session or module on poster paper. State the goal, who attends, what they do, what they leave with, and how you would know it worked.

  3. 3

    Step 3. Gallery walk

    15 min12:15 to 12:30

    Post the designs and walk the room. On each poster, leave one question and one suggestion.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Revise

    10 min12:30 to 12:40

    Return to your poster. Answer the questions and take one suggestion.

  5. 5

    Step 5. Test the limit

    15 min12:40 to 12:55

    Faculty development does not resolve structural problems. For each design, name what depends on a chair, a dean, a schedule, or a budget. Then say what evidence you would offer a dean, and what kind it is.

  6. 6

    Step 6. Close

    5 min12:55 to 1:00

    Write the first sentence of the proposal you would send.

You leave with one design, and with a list of what the design depends on outside the room.
