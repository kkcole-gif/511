The commitment this week tests

Commitment 11 · Pedagogy register

Language difference is a resource, not proof of deficiency

What it means for teaching. Students bring linguistic, rhetorical, cultural, technological, and literacy resources to college. Inclusive writing instruction treats multilingualism and language variation as central to learning while making institutional expectations explicit.

Its limit. The commitment requires more than welcoming language.

Kind of evidence. Normative and theoretical.

Open the Commitment 11 page

Tension to hold. Support and standardization.

Reflection Prompt

This commitment is normative. No effect size settles it, and it is no weaker for it. Rosa and Flores bring evidence from sociolinguistics: judgments about language follow the listener's perception of the speaker. What does a classroom owe the language its students already bring? Then return to your Week 1 sort. Six weeks in, which predictions held?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Thursday's discussion.
