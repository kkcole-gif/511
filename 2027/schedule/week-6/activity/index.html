In class · Thursday, September 30 · Week 6

Two readings of one passage

You read one short student passage twice, with two different purposes, and you compare what each reading rewards. The session opens and closes with your Week 1 sort.

Matched to Commitment 11: Language difference is a resource, not proof of deficiency.

Bring

  • Your Week 1 sort.
  • A pen.
  • Two copies of the passage are supplied.

The session, step by step

Six steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

123456
  1. 1

    Step 1. Open the maps

    10 min11:45 to 11:55

    Take out your Week 1 sort. For the commitments from Weeks 2 through 5, mark each prediction: held, or broke.

  2. 2

    Step 2. First reading: errors

    10 min11:55 to 12:05

    With a partner, read the passage. On the first copy, mark every departure from edited academic English. Count your marks.

  3. 3

    Step 3. Second reading: choices

    15 min12:05 to 12:20

    Take the clean copy. Mark each language choice the writer makes. Beside each mark, write what the choice accomplishes for this purpose and this reader.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Compare the two copies

    10 min12:20 to 12:30

    Lay the copies side by side. What did each reading reward? What did each one hide? Which copy would you hand back to the writer?

  5. 5

    Step 5. Whole room

    20 min12:30 to 12:50

    Bring in the readings. Rosa and Flores (2017) argue judgments about language follow the listener's perception of the speaker. Which of your first-reading marks describe the passage, and which describe the reader? Then take the limit of Commitment 11: what would have to change in a rubric for the second reading to count?

  6. 6

    Step 6. Return to the maps

    10 min12:50 to 1:00

    Find Commitment 11 on your Week 1 sort. Which pile did you place it in? The commitment is normative, and no effect size settles it. Write one sentence on the kind of evidence Rosa and Flores add.

You leave with two annotated copies of one passage and an updated Week 1 sort.
