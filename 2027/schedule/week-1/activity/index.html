Analog card sort

You sort the twelve commitments twice before you study them: once by how firmly you hold each one, and once by the kind of evidence you predict stands behind it. The sort is a record. You check it in Week 6 and again in Week 16.

Matched to all twelve commitments and the three conditions.

Bring

  • A pen.
  • The cards and the record sheet are supplied.

Four kinds of evidence

Causal
An experiment, or a meta-analysis of experiments, tests an effect.
Observational
A study counts or measures what exists and assigns nothing.
Qualitative
A study interprets cases, texts, or interviews.
Normative
A position argues what ought to hold.

Three registers

Activity
What writers do.
Pedagogy
How writing is taught.
Method
How writing produces knowledge.

The session, step by step

Seven steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

1234567
  1. 1

    Step 1. Form a group and take a set

    5 min11:45 to 11:50

    Sit in a group of three or four. Take one set of twelve cards. Each card carries one commitment and the first line of its limit.

  2. 2

    Step 2. First sort: how firmly do you hold it?

    15 min11:50 to 12:05

    Lay the cards in one line across the table, from the commitment your group holds most firmly to the one it doubts most. Talk as you place each card. Where the group splits, turn the card sideways.

  3. 3

    Step 3. Record the line

    5 min12:05 to 12:10

    Copy the order onto the record sheet and mark the sideways cards. Take a photo if you like.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Second sort: what kind of evidence stands behind it?

    20 min12:10 to 12:30

    Shuffle the cards. Sort them into four piles: causal, observational, qualitative, and normative. Predict. Do not look anything up. Beside each commitment on the record sheet, write the register you place it in: activity, pedagogy, or method.

  5. 5

    Step 5. Record the piles

    5 min12:30 to 12:35

    Write each pile onto the record sheet.

  6. 6

    Step 6. Compare across the room

    20 min12:35 to 12:55

    Each group reports two things: the card it argued over longest, and its fullest pile. Listen for a pattern. Do the commitments you placed in the activity register sit in the causal pile?

  7. 7

    Step 7. Keep your predictions

    5 min12:55 to 1:00

    Copy your group's two sorts into your own notes, or keep the photo. Bring them to class in Week 6 and in Week 16.

You leave with two sorts on one sheet: what you hold, and what you predict the evidence is.
