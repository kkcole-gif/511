The commitments this week tests

All twelve commitments and the three conditions, read as a set, with the three registers as the instrument for sorting them.

Open the twelve commitments and the three conditions. Open the three registers.

Reflection Prompt

Sort the twelve before you study them. Which do you hold firmly, and which do you doubt? Place each one in a register: activity, pedagogy, or method. Then predict the kind of evidence standing behind it: causal, observational, qualitative, or normative. Keep your predictions. We check them all semester.

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
