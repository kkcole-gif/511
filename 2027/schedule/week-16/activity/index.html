Portfolio roundtable

You sit with colleagues from other Workshop Teams, who have not read your work. Each of you presents your Portfolio Introduction and one artifact, and the table responds. They are the first outside readers your Introduction is written for.

Matched to all twelve commitments and the three conditions.

Bring

  • Your Portfolio Doc, open to the Introduction tab.
  • One artifact from the portfolio you want outside readers to see.

The protocol

  1. State what you understood the piece's purpose and audience to be.
  2. Name the strongest move already in the draft.
  3. Name one place where you needed more as a reader.
  4. Offer one suggestion tied to a stated criterion.
  5. Leave the decision with the writer.
