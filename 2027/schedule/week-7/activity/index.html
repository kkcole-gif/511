In class · Thursday, October 7 · Week 7

Norming

You respond to the same sample papers as your teammates, and then you compare what each of you called strong, weak, or unclear. Nothing is due.

Matched to Commitment 5: Revision is re-seeing, not merely proofreading and the assessment condition: Assessment is local, multiple, and purpose-driven.

Bring

  • A pen, or a laptop for commenting.
  • The sample ENG 101 papers are in the shared course folder and on paper in class.

The session, step by step

Five steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

12345
  1. 1

    Step 1. Respond alone

    25 min11:45 to 12:10

    Read two sample ENG 101 papers. Respond to each with a different approach: margin comments on one, a single end comment on the other. Write to the student.

  2. 2

    Step 2. Compare in your team

    20 min12:10 to 12:30

    Take one paper at a time. Each reader says what they called strong, weak, and unclear. On the norming sheet, record where the team agreed and where it split.

  3. 3

    Step 3. Trace one split

    10 min12:30 to 12:40

    Pick one disagreement. Ask each reader what they were valuing. Name the criterion behind each judgment.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Whole room

    15 min12:40 to 12:55

    Each team reports one split and the criteria behind it. Place your comments on Hattie and Timperley's levels: task, process, or self-regulation. Which level did the room write at most?

  5. 5

    Step 5. Keep everything

    5 min12:55 to 1:00

    Put your annotated samples and your notes on the splits where you will find them in Week 13. Checkpoint 3 of your Assignment Sequence draws on both.

You leave with annotated samples and a record of where your team's judgments split.
