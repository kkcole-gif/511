The commitment and the condition this week tests

Commitment 5 · Activity register

Revision is re-seeing, not merely proofreading

What it means for teaching. Revision includes reconsidering a writer's questions, purposes, organization, evidence, warrants, audiences, genre choices, and stakes. Editing matters, and it is one dimension of revision.

Its limit. Not every assignment merits multiple drafts.

Kind of evidence. Causal for feedback and for peer feedback in higher education.

Open the Commitment 5 page

Condition · Pedagogy and method registers

Assessment is local, multiple, and purpose-driven

What it means for teaching. The field rejects establishing writing quality through a single timed sample, a universal rubric, a standardized test, or an error count. The alternative is contextual, transparent, multiple, and equitable assessment, designed locally for stated learning goals.

Its limit. The alternative is not the absence of standards.

Kind of evidence. Professional policy, plus causal evidence on feedback.

Open the page for the assessment condition

Tension to hold. Writing for learning and writing for assessment.

Reflection Prompt

Two kinds of claim meet this week. Huisman et al. and Hattie and Timperley report measured effects of feedback. CCCC states a professional position on assessment. Which is which, and what does each license you to say to a chair who asks for proof?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
