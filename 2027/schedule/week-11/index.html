The commitments this week tests

Commitment 8 · Method register

Writing development is lifelong and context-dependent

What it means for teaching. No single course teaches students to write once and for all. Programs distribute meaningful writing experiences across curricular pathways, with increasing complexity and opportunities for transfer.

Its limit. The corrective to the one-course solution should not become an unfunded mandate for all faculty.

Kind of evidence. Qualitative, longitudinal, and programmatic.

Commitment 9 · Method register

Transfer is possible but not automatic

What it means for teaching. Students carry forward genre awareness, rhetorical concepts, strategies, habits of reflection, and prior writing knowledge, and they must recognize when and how these apply in a new situation. Reflection, comparison, metacognition, and explicit attention to differences across contexts support transfer.

Its limit. Transfer should not be framed as a student deficit.

Kind of evidence. Theoretically compelling and empirically thin.
