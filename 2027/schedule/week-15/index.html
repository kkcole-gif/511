The commitment this week tests

Commitment 12 · Activity and pedagogy registers

Writing and communication are multimodal and technologically mediated

What it means for teaching. Writing takes print, digital, visual, audio, spatial, data-rich, and networked forms. Instruction addresses how modes, platforms, tools, accessibility, circulation, and audiences reshape rhetorical decisions.

Its limit. The commitment should not be technologically deterministic, and multimodal work is not inherently engaging or equitable.

Kind of evidence. Theoretical and qualitative.

Open the Commitment 12 page

Reflection Prompt

Research on AI-mediated writing is too young to show generative AI improves transferable writing ability, disciplinary learning, or equity. Institutional confidence is running ahead of the evidence. Read each policy statement as a claim: which register, what kind of evidence, and whose priorities does it protect?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
