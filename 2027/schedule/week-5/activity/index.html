In class · Thursday, September 23 · Week 5

Artifact stations

You read three genres from three disciplines as a newcomer would, and you name the intellectual work each one does.

Matched to Commitment 6: Writing develops through use in consequential contexts and Commitment 7: Disciplines are ways of knowing, doing, and writing.

Suggested form. This week's Workshop Team designs and leads the session, and the team is free to adapt every step.

Bring

  • A pen.
  • The team supplies the printed genres and the station sheets.

The session, step by step

Seven steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

1234567
  1. 1

    Step 1. Set up

    5 min11:45 to 11:50

    Form three groups, one at each station. Each station holds one printed genre from one discipline, such as a lab report, a design brief, or a policy memo.

  2. 2

    Step 2. Station one

    12 min11:50 to 12:02

    Answer four questions on the station sheet. Who writes this, and for whom? What counts as evidence here? What does the form make the writer do? What would a newcomer get wrong?

  3. 3

    Step 3. Station two

    12 min12:02 to 12:14

    Rotate. Answer the same four questions. Write your own answers first, then read the notes the last group left.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Station three

    12 min12:14 to 12:26

    Rotate once more and answer the four questions.

  5. 5

    Step 5. Compare the stations

    14 min12:26 to 12:40

    Back in the whole room, take one question at a time. Where did the three genres give three different answers?

  6. 6

    Step 6. Test the claim and its limit

    15 min12:40 to 12:55

    Carter (2007) calls writing a metadoing. Name one place where a genre gave form to a way of doing. Then name one place where an artifact hid a disagreement inside its discipline.

  7. 7

    Step 7. Close

    5 min12:55 to 1:00

    Write down the one genre from your own field you would add as a fourth station, and the work it does.

You leave with four questions you are able to put to any genre, in any discipline.
