The condition this week tests

Condition · Method register

Class size is a pedagogical and labor condition

What it means for teaching. CCCC recommends 15 or fewer students per writing class, a maximum of 20, and no more than 60 writing students per instructor per term. Enrollment, assignment quantity and genre, feedback expectations, modality, staffing, accessibility obligations, and compensation must be treated together.

Its limit. These figures are norms and policy recommendations grounded in workload analysis and professional standards.

Kind of evidence. Professional norm, plus one experiment in online courses finding little average effect.

Open the page for the labor condition

Tension to hold. Efficiency and educational quality.

Reflection Prompt

The 15/20/60 standard is a professional norm. The one experiment on the reading list found little average effect of class size in online courses. Is the norm weaker for it? Say what kind of instrument a workload norm is, and what it is the right instrument for.

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
