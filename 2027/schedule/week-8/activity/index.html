Case arithmetic

You test one program's staffing numbers against the 15/20/60 standard and compute what one student receives. In the second half, your own Workshop Team reviews Checkpoint 2 of your Assignment Sequence.

Matched to the labor condition: Class size is a pedagogical and labor condition.

Suggested form. This week's Workshop Team designs and leads the session, and the team is free to adapt every step.

Steps 1 through 4 are the team's to adapt. Steps 5 through 7 are the Checkpoint 2 review, and every team runs them as written.

Bring

  • Checkpoint 2: your one-page lesson and your revised prompt, on paper or on screen.
  • A calculator or a phone.

The 15/20/60 standard

CCCC recommends 15 or fewer students per writing class, a maximum of 20, and no more than 60 writing students per instructor per term.

The protocol

  1. State what you understood the piece's purpose and audience to be.
  2. Name the strongest move already in the draft.
  3. Name one place where you needed more as a reader.
  4. Offer one suggestion tied to a stated criterion.
  5. Leave the decision with the writer.

Seven steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

    Step 3. Compute the feedback hours

Then hold the instructor's hours fixed and compute the minutes of response one student receives at 15, 20, and 25 students per section.
