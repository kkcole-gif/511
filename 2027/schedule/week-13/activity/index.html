In class · Thursday, November 18 · Week 13

Seminar discussion

No separate activity this Thursday. The room works the Reflection Prompt against Dolmage (2008).

Matched to Commitment 12: Writing and communication are multimodal and technologically mediated.

Bring

  • Your Perusall annotations for Thursday's reading.
  • Checkpoint 3: your rubric, open or on paper.
  • One passage from Dolmage you want the room to read closely.

How the discussion runs

Three moves, in order. No clock.

  1. 1

    Move 1. Start from the prompt

    Write for three minutes in answer to the Reflection Prompt. Then say your answer to the person beside you.

  2. 2

    Move 2. Place the reading

    Dolmage asks who a classroom is designed for by default. Name the register he works in and the kind of evidence he offers.

  3. 3

    Move 3. Turn to your rubric

    Read one row of your rubric with Dolmage's question in hand. Who does the row assume? Mark one change.

Reflection Prompt

The limit of this commitment is access. Multimodal work is not equitable on its own. Dolmage asks who a classroom is designed for by default. Which register is he working in, and what does his kind of evidence let you claim about your own rubric?

You leave with one marked change to your rubric, and a reason for it.
