Write, pass, write

You write to think, a reader answers, and you write again. Then your writing group hears one excerpt from each Literacy Narrative.

Matched to Commitment 3: Writing is a mode of learning and inquiry.

Bring

  • Paper and a pen.
  • One excerpt from your Literacy Narrative draft, a paragraph or two, to read aloud.

The protocol

  1. State what you understood the piece's purpose and audience to be.
  2. Name the strongest move already in the draft.
  3. Name one place where you needed more as a reader.
  4. Offer one suggestion tied to a stated criterion.
  5. Leave the decision with the writer.

The session, step by step

Seven steps in 75 minutes, 11:45 AM to 1:00 PM. Each block below is one step, sized by its minutes.

1234567
  1. 1

    Step 1. Write

    5 min11:45 to 11:50

    Answer the question on the board in a freewrite on paper. Keep the pen moving for five minutes. Do not stop to fix a sentence.

  2. 2

    Step 2. Pass

    8 min11:50 to 11:58

    Trade pages with a partner. Read the freewrite once. In the margin, underline the sentence closest to a claim, and ask one question the writer has not answered.

  3. 3

    Step 3. Write again

    10 min11:58 to 12:08

    Take your page back and read the margin. Under the freewrite, write one claim in two or three sentences. Make it a claim a colleague would be able to dispute.

  4. 4

    Step 4. Hear the claims

    12 min12:08 to 12:20

    Volunteers read a claim aloud. The room names what each claim would need as evidence.

  5. 5

    Step 5. Name what the writing did

    10 min12:20 to 12:30

    Which step moved your thinking: the freewrite, the reader's margin, or the revision? Hold your answer against the week's evidence. Bangert-Drowns, Hurley, and Wilkinson (2004) found a small positive average effect across 48 studies. The commitment's limit says the value of writing depends on the design of the task and on the response it receives.

  6. 6

    Step 6. Writing group

    25 min12:30 to 12:55

    Sit with your Workshop Team. Each writer reads one excerpt from the Literacy Narrative aloud. The team answers with the first three steps of the protocol: the purpose and audience you heard, the strongest move, and one place where you needed more.

  7. 7

    Step 7. Close

    5 min12:55 to 1:00

    Write down the one response you plan to act on before Sunday.

You leave with a claim on paper and one response to act on before Sunday, September 12.
