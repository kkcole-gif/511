The commitment this week tests

Commitment 4 · Activity register

Writing is recursive, developmental, and processual

What it means for teaching. Planning, drafting, researching, reading, composing, revising, editing, and reflecting are interdependent, not linear stages. Courses create time and structures for substantive revision.

Its limit. “Process” does not reduce to a uniform sequence.

Kind of evidence. Causal for strategy instruction, tested with adolescent writers.

Open the Commitment 4 page

Tension to hold. Process and product.

Reflection Prompt

This is the activity register's strongest evidence. Graham and Perin tested strategy instruction with adolescent writers, not college students. What carries over to a college classroom, and what kind of evidence would you need before saying so?

The prompt guides the reflection in your Perusall annotation, and it opens Tuesday's discussion.
