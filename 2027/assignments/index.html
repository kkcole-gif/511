Five assignments · three registers

Assignments

The course has five assignments, scaled to the three registers. You annotate every week. You write a Literacy Narrative and a first Teaching Philosophy as a writer. You build one Assignment Sequence for a classroom, in three checkpoints. Your team leads one workshop and argues one Program Memo at the scale of a program. The Pedagogy Statement and Portfolio Doc gather the work in Week 16.

Each assignment opens with its register, its scale, the commitments it tests, and the practice it enacts. All individual work goes in your Portfolio Doc.
