Portfolio Doc Setup

All individual work lives in one place: your Portfolio Doc, a single Google Doc in the shared class folder. You build it tab by tab. By Week 16, the final project is a revision of what the tabs already hold.

Set it up once, before Week 3

  1. Open the shared class folder and sign in with your NC State Google account. The folder link arrives in Moodle in Week 1.
  2. Inside the folder, create a new Google Doc. Name it exactly Lastname_ENG511_Portfolio_2027. Use your last name only, with underscores and no spaces.
  3. Add four tabs with Insert → Add tab. Name them exactly as the table below names them. Your teammates and I go to these tabs to respond.
  4. Share the doc with your Workshop Team once teams form, with permission to comment.
  5. Stop there. You add writing to each tab as its assignment comes due.

The four tabs

Tab What goes in it When
Introduction Your Portfolio Introduction, written last. Week 16
Literacy Narrative Your Literacy Narrative and its Commitments Close. Week 3, revisited in Week 16
Assignment Sequence The prompt, the lesson, the rubric and feedback plan, and then the complete sequence with its rationale. Earlier versions stay visible below. Checkpoints in Weeks 6, 8, and 13. Complete in Week 15
Pedagogy Statement Your Teaching Philosophy Draft, and later the Composition Pedagogy Statement with its tagged sources. The draft stays visible below the statement. Week 3 and Week 16

Why one doc with tabs

Your Week 3 Teaching Philosophy Draft and your Week 16 Pedagogy Statement sit in one tab, so the distance between them is on the page. The same holds for each version of your Assignment Sequence. Commitment 5 holds revision is re-seeing, and the tabs keep what you saw first.

Keep earlier versions visible

Revise at the top of the tab. Leave the earlier version below it, under a clear header with its week.

Who reads it

Your Workshop Team comments in the tabs at every checkpoint. I read closely and respond in writing twice: to the complete Assignment Sequence in Week 15 and to the finished Portfolio Doc in Week 16.

If you need more time

Write to me before the deadline. An extension you ask for in advance carries no penalty. A pattern of late work with no word from you affects the labor record.

Questions

Email me at kkcole2@ncsu.edu with ENG 511 in the subject line, or book office hours. For a question about an assignment, write at least 48 hours before the due date.
