Labor-based assessment

Grading

In this course, grading prioritizes learning over letter grades. Individual assignments carry no grades. You receive structured response from your Workshop Team at each checkpoint, and written response from me on the complete Assignment Sequence (Week 15) and the finished Portfolio Doc (Week 16). Your final course grade is based on your engagement, effort, and work completed. Grades are tracked using the Graduate Bulletin descriptors (Excellent, Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, Unacceptable).

Grade Expected labor
A Full participation in all discussions, weekly readings, and peer responses. Complete all assignments with engaged critical synthesis, collaborative discussion, and deep reflection.
B Participate in at least 85% of discussions, responses, and assignments.
C Participate in at least 75% of discussions and assignments.
D/F Participation below 75% and/or missing assignments.

What the percentages count

The percentages count four things together: class sessions attended, Perusall annotations completed, team responses given, and assignments submitted.

When the labor expectation is met

For a team-reviewed assignment, the labor expectation is met when three things are true:

  1. Your complete draft reached your team by the posting date.
  2. You responded to each teammate's draft using the protocol.
  3. Your final version is in its tab by the Sunday deadline.

What I read

I confirm each exchange happened and the labor expectation was met. Your team responds at every checkpoint, and I write no comments there. I read closely and respond in writing twice: to the complete Assignment Sequence in Week 15 and to the finished Portfolio Doc in Week 16.
