The course

This course is built on twelve commitments the field of writing studies holds as contested but durable, and on three conditions instruction requires. Each week takes one or two. You read the scholarship behind the claim and the critique at its edge.

Commitments

The twelve claims and the three conditions, each with its limit and its evidence.

Registers

Activity, pedagogy, and method: the three ways this course reads the word “writing.”

Schedule

Sixteen weeks on one page, each with its activity and what is due.

Assignments

Five assignments, one for each scale the registers name: writer, classroom, program.

Readings

Full citations with links, from inside the field and from outside it.

Course guide

Syllabus, grading, Perusall, Portfolio Doc setup, teams, and terms.
