Today's Reading

"Composition at the Turn of the 21st Century"
Richard Fulkerson — 2005

From the Reading Discussion

"Choosing a pedagogy means choosing what writing is for. Two instructors running identical activities — peer review, discussion, collaborative drafting — could be working toward completely different versions of a successful writer."

"The field has diverged before. It seems like a natural part of any academic discipline. If Fulkerson revisited this study today, the finding would likely be: it has diverged again."

"An exclusive genre approach risks sliding into formalism — writing as product rather than process — without intending to."

"Complete ideological neutrality in the classroom seems impossible. The more honest question may be how much space students get to challenge the teacher's values, not whether those values are present."
